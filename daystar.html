About Us

Daystar University is a chartered private university in Kenya, known for producing graduates who combine strong academic foundations with values-based leadership. Our campuses in Athi River and Nairobi provide a vibrant learning environment supported by dedicated faculty and modern facilities.

Academic Programmes

  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Commerce
  • Bachelor of Education (Arts)
  • Master of Arts in Marriage and Family Therapy
  • Bachelor of Science in International Business Administration
